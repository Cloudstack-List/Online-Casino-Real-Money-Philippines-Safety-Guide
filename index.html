Legal Status: Is Online Casino Legal in the Philippines?

PAGCOR’s Role & Licensing

PAGCOR (Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation) oversees and regulates gaming operations in the Philippines. To legally run or host online casino games here, an operator must hold a PAGCOR e-Gaming license (or be in compliance with local rules).
Offshore casinos may operate under foreign licenses, but players should still ensure those sites are permitted for Filipino use and follow best practices.

What Laws Apply to Players

  • There is no law that criminalizes a Filipinos’ playing in offshore casino websites, unless gambling is conducted without proper regulation or violates local restrictions.
  • However, operators must follow local rules, and payment processors/banks may block suspicious transactions.
  • Always check whether a casino is approved for use by Filipino players. Many Philippines-focused sites clarify this clearly.

2025 Updates & Enforcement

In 2025, PAGCOR has stepped up scrutiny of local operations and is pushing for stricter compliance on local payment methods, KYC (know your customer) rules, and anti-money laundering controls.
Operators failing to meet standards may be blocked or fined.

Tax & Reporting: What You Must Know

Are Casino Winnings Taxable in the Philippines?

Yes. Winnings from games of chance (including casino play) are generally subject to taxation under Philippine law. The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) expects players to report significant winnings as part of their income.

Tax Rates & Withholding

  • Operators may withhold a portion of large payouts as taxes on the player’s behalf.
  • The standard rate for prizes and winnings in PH often hovers around 20% (depending on updates).
  • Check latest BIR regulations in 2024–2025 to confirm rates.

How to Report

  • Include your gambling winnings on your annual income tax return (ITR).
  • Keep documentation (receipts, screenshots) of big wins to support your declaration.
  • Consult a tax adviser for large or frequent winnings.
